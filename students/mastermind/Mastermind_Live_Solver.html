How the two strategies work

Both finish within five guesses when followed from a fresh game. Our Knuth implementation starts with two pairs and averages 4.476080 guesses. Koyama–Lai starts with a pair and two different colours and averages 4.341049. Both figures include the final correct guess and were checked over all 1,296 secrets.

You can enter any guesses and switch strategy without losing clues. Koyama–Lai uses its decision table when the remaining candidate set is covered; otherwise it falls back to Knuth-style minimax. Arbitrary guesses or switching strategies do not carry the original five-guess guarantee.
