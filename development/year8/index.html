For adults: choosing the right level

These are suggested entry points, not ability labels. A learner may explore the model long before they can analyse it formally. Use the starting question first; the deeper challenge is optional. A completed game is not evidence of curriculum mastery.

Activities marked enrichment connect to the curriculum but may introduce ideas beyond this stage. The questions here are original and independent of White Rose Education.

What comes next?

Further workshops are still needed for ratio and proportion, equations, standard form, probability models and statistical displays.

These activities supplement teaching and practice. They do not yet form a complete course or assessment system.
