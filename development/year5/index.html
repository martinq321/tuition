For parents and educators

Start with a manageable example. Ask the learner to make it, explain it in their own way, then connect it to a written calculation. Use the hint when useful; a hint is support, not failure. Revisit the idea after a gap before judging understanding.

The block order follows White Rose's published Year 5 version 3 overview. These are original independent activities, not official White Rose resources or copied worksheets. They support exploration and consolidation alongside teaching; they do not yet cover every small step.

Each workshop has a local record, a reflection and a download option. These are saved on this browser, not to secure pupil accounts. Completion does not establish mastery.

For adults: choosing the right level

These are suggested entry points, not ability labels. A learner may explore the model long before they can analyse it formally. Use the starting question first; the deeper challenge is optional. A completed game is not evidence of curriculum mastery.

Activities marked enrichment connect to the curriculum but may introduce ideas beyond this stage. The questions here are original and independent of White Rose Education.
