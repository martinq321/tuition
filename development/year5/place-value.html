For parents and educators · teaching notes and sources

Choose the smaller range if helpful, then extend to one million. Use labelled counters and paper place-value mats. Ask the learner to explain each digit’s value and exchange ten counters for one in the next place. Check whether the learner distinguishes a digit from its value and understands the purpose of zero.

Keep practical exploration, reasoning and fluency together. Use a few independent questions after the activity, then revisit after a gap. Offer an extension through explaining all possibilities, not simply larger numbers.
