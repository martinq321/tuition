Probability and data

Chance garden

Statistics · simulation and expectation · enrichment context

Start here
Record the distribution of coin totals on a fixed day across many runs.

Investigate and go further

Investigate
Estimate the mean and variance and compare batches. State assumptions about the random rule.

Deeper challenge
Derive expected growth using conditional expectation if ready; this extension goes beyond the standard course.

Off screen
Spreadsheet or code for repeated trials.

Curves and motion

Curve lab

Mechanics · energy and modelling · enrichment

Start here
Compare ramp times and discuss the model’s assumptions.

Investigate and go further

Investigate
For a particle released from rest without friction, use v² = 2gh to relate speed to vertical drop.

Deeper challenge
Explain why finding the fastest curve is a calculus-of-variations problem beyond A-level. Do not treat the simulation as a proof.

Off screen
Paper for a particle model; real rolling marbles also have rotational energy.

For adults: choosing the right level

These are suggested entry points, not ability labels. A learner may explore the model long before they can analyse it formally. Use the starting question first; the deeper challenge is optional. A completed game is not evidence of curriculum mastery.

Activities marked enrichment connect to the curriculum but may introduce ideas beyond this stage. The questions here are original and independent of White Rose Education.

What comes next?

This is an investigation collection, not a complete course. Functions, trigonometry, exponentials and logarithms, integration, vectors, distributions, hypothesis tests, forces and kinematics need dedicated teaching sequences.

These activities supplement teaching and practice. They do not yet form a complete course or assessment system.
