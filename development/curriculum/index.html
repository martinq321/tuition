Curriculum framing

Collections are mapped to England’s mathematics programme and qualification subject content. Years 7–9 are a suggested sequence within KS3. GCSE tier labels describe these investigations, not a complete exam specification. Formal A-level analysis often needs paper, a spreadsheet or a graphing tool alongside the activity.

Original activity prompts use a small-step approach: explore, connect representations, explain and extend. These resources are independent of White Rose Education.

  • DfE: primary and secondary mathematics
  • DfE: GCSE mathematics subject content
  • DfE: AS and A-level mathematics subject content
  • White Rose: secondary small-step sequencing

Evidence of learning comes from the learner’s explanation and transfer to a fresh problem, not just completing an activity.
