Year 3 · Autumn

Place value to 1,000

Learning objectives

  • Read and write numbers to 1,000 in figures and words. Build three-digit numbers and explain how zero holds a place in 405 or 450.
  • Estimate and compare positions on partly labelled lines. Order numbers by comparing hundreds, tens and ones and use this to solve a practical problem.
  • Count from zero in 50s and 100s. Find 10 or 100 more or less, including across a hundreds boundary.
