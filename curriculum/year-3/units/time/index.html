Curriculum / Year 3

Year 3 · Summer

Clocks, calendars and duration

Learning objectives

  • Estimate and read an analogue clock to the nearest minute, including faces marked I to XII. Write the time and distinguish am, pm, noon and midnight.
  • Match a time of day to its 12-hour and 24-hour readings. Use morning, afternoon or evening to resolve an ambiguous time.
  • Use 60 seconds in a minute and record durations in seconds, minutes or hours. Compare events and work out how much longer one took.
  • Use the number of days in each month and the 365- or 366-day year to answer calendar problems.
