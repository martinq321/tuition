Year 3 · Spring

Length and perimeter

Learning objectives

  • Choose metres, centimetres or millimetres and measure accurately with an appropriate tool.
  • Compare lengths and add or subtract them after expressing them in the same unit.
  • Trace the complete boundary of a simple flat shape, measure its sides and add the lengths to find its perimeter.

Try an investigation

  • 36 fences

    Build two different rectangles with the same fence. Count around each boundary to check its perimeter.
