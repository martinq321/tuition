Year 3 · Spring

Fractions as numbers and tenths

Learning objectives

  • Divide a whole into equal parts and locate unit and non-unit fractions with small denominators on a number line.
  • Make tenths by dividing a whole or a one-digit quantity by ten. Count forwards and backwards in tenths and explain where ten tenths lands.
  • Compare and order unit fractions using the same whole, then compare fractions with a common denominator.
  • Use matching lengths or areas to show equivalent fractions with small denominators and explain how the pictures represent equal amounts.
