Year 3 · Autumn

The 3, 4 and 8 times tables

Learning objectives

  • Count from zero in 4s and 8s. Double groups of two to make fours and double fours to make eights.
  • Use equal groups or an array to build the 3, 4 and 8 tables, then recall products and related division facts.
  • Use a known table fact to find a missing factor or quotient and explain how you checked it.

Try an investigation

  • Times tables

    Build an array for a three-times-table fact. Read both a multiplication and a division from it.

    Theme: Products and factors
  • Number patterns

    Mark multiples of four in the number square. Predict the next two and explain the pattern.

    Theme: Patterns and change
