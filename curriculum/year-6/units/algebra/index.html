Curriculum / Year 6

Year 6 · Spring

Algebra

Learning objectives

  • Use letters for unknown numbers and write a missing-number problem as an equation.
  • Substitute values into a simple formula, keeping track of the units in the result.
  • Generate a linear number sequence from a rule and describe the constant change between terms.
  • Find pairs satisfying an equation with two unknowns. State the allowed values and list all possible combinations systematically when the choices are finite.

Try an investigation

  • Take-away strategy

    Play with a maximum take of three. List the losing starting numbers for the player to move under the last-counter-wins rule and describe a pattern.

    Theme: Winning positions
  • 15 game

    Find all triples of different numbers from 1 to 9 that total 15. Organise them so a partner can check for repeats.

    Theme: Winning positions
