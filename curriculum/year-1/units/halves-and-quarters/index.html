Year 1 · Summer

Halves and quarters

Learning objectives

  • Fold a shape or divide an object into two equal parts. Name one part as a half and explain why an uneven split fails.
  • Make four equal parts of a shape and name one as a quarter. Find another way to divide the same whole into quarters.
  • Share a collection into two or four equal groups to find half or a quarter of a quantity.
