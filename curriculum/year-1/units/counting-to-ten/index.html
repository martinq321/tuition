Year 1 · Autumn

Counting and comparing to 10

Learning objectives

  • Match one number word to each object. Rearrange the objects and explain why the total stays the same.
  • Read and write numerals from 0 to 10, match them to collections and find them on a number line.
  • Find one more or one fewer. Match objects in pairs to decide which collection has more, fewer or the same number.
  • Order three collections from least to most and explain how you know.
