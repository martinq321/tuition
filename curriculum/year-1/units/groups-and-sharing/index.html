Year 1 · Summer

Equal groups, multiplication and division

Learning objectives

  • Make pairs and groups of five or ten. Count in 2s, 5s and 10s and match the spoken count to the total number of objects.
  • With a teacher, arrange equal groups into rows in an array and draw a picture to solve a one-step multiplication story.
  • Solve a one-step division story by sharing objects fairly or making groups of a given size. Explain what the final count tells you.
