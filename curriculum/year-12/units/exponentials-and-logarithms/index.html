Year 12 · Summer · Pure

Exponentials, logarithms and models

Learning objectives

  • Sketch exponential graphs for growth and decay and their inverse logarithms. For a base a > 0 with a ≠ 1, state that logₐ x has domain x > 0 and range ℝ. Relate ln x to eˣ.
  • Use the product, quotient and power laws of logarithms to solve exponential equations and express exact solutions.
  • Explain how the gradient of eᵏˣ is linked to its value. Fit and interpret growth or decay models, with examples such as compound interest or radioactive decay.
  • Choose log–log or semi-log axes to turn a power law or exponential relationship into a straight line, and recover both model parameters from the line.
  • Test the fitted model against the original data. Describe a range of plausible inputs and how a changing growth rate would affect a prediction.
