Year 12 · Spring · Pure

Gradients, derivatives and optimisation

Learning objectives

  • Approach a tangent gradient using a sequence of secants. Use a difference quotient to derive derivatives of small positive integer powers.
  • Differentiate a sum of rational powers and sketch its derivative from the original graph. Interpret the derivative as an instantaneous rate with units.
  • Find tangents and normals, including at a horizontal tangent, and determine where a function increases or decreases.
  • Find and classify stationary points using signs or second derivatives. Compare end points and stationary points when solving an optimisation problem on a restricted interval.
