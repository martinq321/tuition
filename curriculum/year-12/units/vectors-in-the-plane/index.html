Year 12 · Summer · Pure

Vectors in the plane

Learning objectives

  • Move between components, a diagram, and magnitude with direction. Form unit vectors and scale or add vectors geometrically and algebraically.
  • Use position vectors to find the displacement and distance between two points, and divide a line segment in a given ratio.
  • Build a vector argument for collinearity or parallelism and apply the same operations to a displacement or a set of forces.
