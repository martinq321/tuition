Year 12 · Summer · Mechanics

Forces, equilibrium and connected particles

Learning objectives

  • Draw a separate force diagram for each body. Identify weight, normal reaction, tension and applied forces, and distinguish a Newton’s third-law pair acting on different bodies.
  • Use the first and second laws to distinguish equilibrium from acceleration. Combine forces as vectors and write equations for straight-line motion.
  • Connect the equations for particles joined by a light inextensible string, including a smooth pulley, and explain how the assumptions relate accelerations and tensions.
  • Differentiate displacement or integrate acceleration and velocity in straight-line motion. Use initial conditions and split the calculation at reversals when finding distance.
