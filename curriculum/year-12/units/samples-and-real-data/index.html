Year 12 · Autumn · Statistics

Sampling and the large data set

Learning objectives

  • Frame a question about a population. Design a sample and compare simple random and opportunity sampling with systematic, stratified or quota approaches.
  • Explain how coverage, bias and sampling variability affect an inference. Compare conclusions from different samples of the same population.
  • Explore the exam board’s large data set in a spreadsheet. Identify what each row represents, variable types, units, missing-value codes and plausible ranges.
  • Clean a subset and record how errors and unusual values were handled. Compare groups with box plots, cumulative frequencies or histograms. For unequal class widths, use frequency density as the bar height so that bar area represents frequency.
  • Calculate mean and standard deviation from data and summary totals, checking the result with a calculator. Choose a suitable measure of centre and spread for the comparison.
