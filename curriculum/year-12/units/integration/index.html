Year 12 · Summer · Pure

Antiderivatives and signed area

Learning objectives

  • Reverse differentiation for sums of rational powers other than x⁻¹. Use the integration constant and a known point to recover a particular function.
  • Use the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to connect accumulated area with an antiderivative and to evaluate a definite integral.
  • Split an interval at crossings of the axis to find total geometric area. Check the result against a sketch.
  • Reconstruct a quantity from its rate of change, retaining its initial value and interpreting the units of the accumulated quantity.

Specification references H1, H2, H3
