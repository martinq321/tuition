Year 12 · Autumn · Pure

Quadratics, intersections and inequalities

Learning objectives

  • Relate the factorised, expanded and completed-square forms of a quadratic to its roots and turning point. Use the discriminant to decide how many real intersections to expect.
  • Recognise an equation that becomes quadratic after a substitution, solve it and check the solutions in the original equation.
  • Use elimination or substitution to solve simultaneous equations, including a line meeting a parabola.
  • Solve inequalities involving linear or quadratic expressions, brackets and numerical fractions. Record the solution with intervals or sets and match it to a graph.
  • Shade regions defined by linear or quadratic inequalities and show whether the boundary belongs to the solution.

Specification references B3, B4, B5, B7
