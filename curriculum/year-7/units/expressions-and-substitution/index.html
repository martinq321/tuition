Year 7 · Autumn

Algebraic expressions and substitution

Learning objectives

  • Translate words and diagrams into expressions. Read 3y, ab, a²b and a/b, and write fractional coefficients and brackets clearly.
  • Identify terms, factors, coefficients and variables. Distinguish an expression from an equation or an inequality.
  • Collect like terms and use substitution to check examples of equivalent expressions.
  • Substitute signed values into simple formulae for familiar quantities and include the correct units in the answer.
