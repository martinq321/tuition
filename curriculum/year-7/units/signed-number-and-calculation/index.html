Year 7 · Autumn

Signed numbers and calculation

Learning objectives

  • Calculate changes above and below zero and distinguish a negative value from a subtraction sign.
  • Add, subtract, multiply and divide signed integers and decimals, choosing an efficient mental or written method.
  • Evaluate expressions containing several operations, brackets and square powers. Explain why changing a bracket can change the result.
  • Check calculations using an inverse operation, an estimate or a second method. Interpret a calculator result in its original units.
