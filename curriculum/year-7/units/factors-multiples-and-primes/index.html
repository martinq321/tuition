Year 7 · Autumn

Factors, multiples and primes

Learning objectives

  • List factors in pairs, identify primes and composites, and explain why 1 belongs to neither group.
  • Find common factors and multiples, including the highest common factor and the lowest common multiple.
  • Write a positive integer greater than 1 as a product of primes, using powers to record repetitions. Compare two factor trees to explain uniqueness up to order.
  • Choose between a common-factor and a common-multiple method for problems about equal groups or recurring events.

Try an investigation

  • Square it up

    Remove equal squares from a rectangle in stages. Explain why a common factor of the side lengths also divides the leftover length.

    Theme: Tiling and packing
  • Divisor game

    List the available proper divisors before choosing a number. Check which of them the other player would receive.

    Theme: Products and factors
