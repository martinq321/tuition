Curriculum / Year 7

Year 7 · Spring

Ratio, sharing and scaling

Learning objectives

  • Compare quantities in the same units and simplify a ratio by dividing each part by a common factor.
  • Distinguish part-to-part from part-to-whole comparisons and convert between the two descriptions.
  • Split a quantity into two parts from either kind of ratio and write the ratio for a completed split.
  • Use a bar model or ratio table to scale a recipe. Explain when a comparison calls for addition and when it calls for multiplication.
