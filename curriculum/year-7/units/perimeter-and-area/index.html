Year 7 · Summer

Perimeter and area

Learning objectives

  • Calculate perimeters of triangles, quadrilaterals and compound shapes, deducing missing side lengths where possible.
  • Derive the areas of a parallelogram and triangle by rearranging or doubling shapes and identify the perpendicular height.
  • Derive the trapezium area formula by pairing two identical trapezia and apply it with consistent units.
  • Decompose a composite region in two ways and check that both methods give the same area. Distinguish linear from square units.
