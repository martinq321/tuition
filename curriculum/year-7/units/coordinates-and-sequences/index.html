Curriculum / Year 7

Year 7 · Spring

Coordinates and sequences

Learning objectives

  • Plot, label and read ordered pairs in all four quadrants on axes with suitable intervals.
  • Generate sequences from a rule linking neighbouring terms or from a rule giving the term at a stated position.
  • Describe and extend arithmetic and geometric patterns, and identify examples with neither a fixed difference nor a fixed ratio.
  • Relate a growing diagram to its table of positions and values. Test a conjecture against more than the first few terms.

Try an investigation

  • Tower of Hanoi

    Solve the two-disc and three-disc puzzles. Record your shortest move counts and predict what adding one disc will change.
