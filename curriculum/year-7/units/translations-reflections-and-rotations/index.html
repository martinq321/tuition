Year 7 · Summer

Translations, reflections and rotations

Learning objectives

  • Draw translations, reflections and rotations of shapes on grids and plain paper.
  • Describe a translation by its displacement, a reflection by its mirror line and a rotation by its centre, angle and direction.
  • Check corresponding lengths and angles, identify which properties stay fixed and relate these transformations to congruence.
  • Find a transformation from an object and its image, and explain when the information supplied allows more than one description.
