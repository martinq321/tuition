Year 7 · Autumn

Place value, decimals and measures

Learning objectives

  • Read, partition and compare integers of any size and decimals, including numbers with leading or internal zeros.
  • Place positive and negative values on scaled number lines and use =, ≠, <, >, ≤ and ≥ to state comparisons.
  • Choose and convert units for lengths, masses, capacities, times and sums of money, including decimal measurements.
  • Round to a chosen place or number of decimal places. Estimate before calculating and judge whether a rounded answer is useful.
