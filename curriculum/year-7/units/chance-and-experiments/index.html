Curriculum / Year 7

Year 7 · Summer

Chance and experiments

Learning objectives

  • Use the scale from zero to one to describe impossible, uncertain and certain events, and compare chances in words and numbers.
  • Distinguish equal and unequal chances. State the assumption that makes a simple theoretical probability calculation valid.
  • List all outcomes of a single trial and use their probabilities, including missing probabilities or complements, to account for a total of one.
  • Record outcomes of repeated trials, calculate relative frequencies and compare the observations with a prediction about fairness.
