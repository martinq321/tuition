Year 9 · Autumn

Real numbers, accuracy and error intervals

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish integers, rational numbers and irrational numbers on the real number line. Show why the sets of integers, rationals and reals are each infinite.
  • Compare exact powers, fractions and roots with decimal approximations, including measurements expressed in standard form.
  • Round to decimal places or significant figures according to a stated purpose and use rounding to estimate a multi-step result.
  • Write the interval of possible original values for a rounded measurement, marking each endpoint correctly under the stated rounding convention.
  • Use simple error intervals to estimate a possible error in a calculated quantity. Keep intermediate calculator values until the final rounding.
