Year 9 · Spring

Pythagoras and geometric deduction

Learning objectives

  • Use an area rearrangement or similar triangles to justify a² + b² = c² for a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse c.
  • Calculate a missing hypotenuse or shorter side, keeping an exact root or rounding appropriately. Check that the longest side is plausible.
  • Use Pythagoras’ theorem to find distances on a coordinate grid and solve problems using more than one right-angled triangle.
  • Combine angle facts, congruence, similarity and quadrilateral properties in short proofs about sides and angles. State the reason for each deduction.
