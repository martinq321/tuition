Year 9 · Spring

Trigonometry in right-angled triangles

Learning objectives

  • Use similar right-angled triangles to explain why ratios of corresponding side lengths depend on the chosen acute angle.
  • Identify opposite and adjacent sides relative to that angle, and select sine, cosine or tangent to calculate a missing side.
  • Find missing acute angles using inverse trigonometric functions in degree mode.
  • Choose between trigonometry and Pythagoras’ theorem for right-triangle problems, draw a labelled model and check the units and size of an answer.
