Curriculum / Year 9

Year 9 · Summer

Multi-step modelling and decision-making

Learning objectives

  • Break an unfamiliar problem into smaller steps and name the quantities, assumptions and units before choosing a method.
  • Compare financial options combining ratio, percentage change, simple interest and a fixed or variable charge.
  • Represent the same decision in a calculation, table and graph, and check that these representations agree.
  • Test how the conclusion changes when a price, measurement or assumption changes. Present a reasoned recommendation with appropriate precision.
