Curriculum / Year 9

Year 9 · Spring

Interpreting graphs and models

Learning objectives

  • Read and sketch piecewise-linear graphs for journeys or tariffs and interpret a change in slope in context.
  • Use supplied exponential and reciprocal graphs to estimate unknown inputs or outputs, selecting an appropriate scale and range.
  • Connect repeated multiplication in a geometric sequence to a growth or decay graph, and distinguish it from constant-difference growth.
  • Translate a situation between a description, table, equation and graph. Explain where interpolation is reasonable and where extrapolation depends on an assumption.
