Curriculum / Year 9

Year 9 · Autumn

Algebraic products, formulae and proof

Learning objectives

  • Expand a product of two binomials, collect like terms, and extend the distributive method to a product of three binomials.
  • Factorise by taking out a common factor and verify equivalence by re-expanding. Connect multiplication of brackets to an area diagram.
  • Rearrange a formula to isolate a chosen variable, including cases that require collecting terms, and test the rearrangement by substitution.
  • Use scientific formulae with consistent units. Represent an arithmetic generalisation algebraically and give a proof or a counterexample.

Try an investigation

  • Painted cubes

    Paint the outside of a cube of side n. Write an expression for the small cubes with exactly two painted faces and justify it edge by edge.
