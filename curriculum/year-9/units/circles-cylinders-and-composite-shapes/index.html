Curriculum / Year 9

Year 9 · Spring

Circles, cylinders and composite shapes

Learning objectives

  • Relate radius, diameter and circumference. Use symmetry and equal radii to justify properties of simple circle diagrams.
  • Calculate circle circumference and area, leave answers in terms of π when appropriate, and distinguish boundary length from enclosed area.
  • Find perimeters and areas of composite figures, including shapes incorporating semicircles, by identifying the actual boundary and any shared edges.
  • Use cross-sectional area and length to derive cylinder volume, and use dimensions or nets to calculate missing measures in composite solids.
