Year 9 · Autumn

Linear and quadratic graphs

Learning objectives

  • Construct and sketch linear and simple quadratic graphs from equations using suitable scales. Identify intercepts, symmetry and turning points where relevant.
  • Estimate outputs from a graph and find possible inputs for a given output, including when a quadratic gives two, one or no real inputs.
  • Read the intersection of two straight lines as an approximate solution of simultaneous linear equations. Check the coordinate in both equations.
  • Explain how the gradient and intercept affect a linear model and interpret a graph crossing as a change in which of two options costs less.
