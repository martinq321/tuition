Year 13 · Summer · Pure

Differential equations and model checking

Learning objectives

  • Translate a rate statement into a first-order differential equation for a population, motion or another changing quantity. Explain the sign, units and meaning of each parameter.
  • Separate variables, integrate and use an initial condition to determine a particular solution. Check for constant solutions that division by a factor could remove.
  • Check a proposed solution by differentiating it and testing the initial conditions. Interpret its long-term behaviour, domain and limitations against the original situation.

Specification references G6, H7, H8, B11
