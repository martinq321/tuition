Year 13 · Autumn · Mechanics

Resolved forces and friction

Learning objectives

  • Choose axes along and perpendicular to a slope or another direction of motion. Resolve weight, reaction, tension and applied forces consistently.
  • Find equilibrium under coplanar forces or apply Newton’s second law to the components of a resultant. Use separate diagrams for connected bodies.
  • Use F ≤ μR for static friction, identify the limiting case, and determine the direction of friction from the possible motion. Use the sliding-friction model where appropriate.
  • Test a proposed state of rest, impending motion or acceleration against the equations and friction bound. Interpret a threshold angle or applied force.
