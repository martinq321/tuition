Year 13 · Autumn · Pure

Radians, trigonometric functions and identities

Learning objectives

  • Convert degrees to radians and find arc lengths and sector areas. Use sin θ ≈ θ, tan θ ≈ θ and cos θ ≈ 1 − θ²/2 for small angles measured in radians.
  • Sketch secant, cosecant and cotangent. For integer k, exclude π/2 + kπ from the domain of secant and kπ from the domains of cosecant and cotangent. Read off |y| ≥ 1 for secant and cosecant and all real y for cotangent. Sketch arcsin, arccos and arctan on their principal branches and state their domains and ranges.
  • Use the reciprocal Pythagorean identities and the sum, difference and double-angle formulae for sine, cosine and tangent. Explain a geometric proof of the addition formulae.
  • Combine a sine and cosine of the same argument into one phase-shifted sinusoid. Find its extreme values and solve equations over a specified interval.
  • Prove trigonometric identities on their valid domains, and use trigonometry to interpret a periodic model or resolve a vector.
