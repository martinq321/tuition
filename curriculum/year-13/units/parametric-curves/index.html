Year 13 · Spring · Pure

Parametric curves and motion

Learning objectives

  • Trace a parametric curve over a specified interval, marking the direction of travel and points reached more than once.
  • Eliminate a parameter to obtain a Cartesian relation, retaining range restrictions. Give a parametrisation for a familiar line or curve.
  • Model two coordinates using a common time parameter. Interpret intersections, allowable times and any discrepancies between the model and the observed path.

Specification references C3, C4, B11
