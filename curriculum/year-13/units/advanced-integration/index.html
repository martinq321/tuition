Curriculum / Year 13

Year 13 · Spring · Pure

Integration techniques and areas between curves

Learning objectives

  • Describe a definite integral as the limit of rectangle sums, then use the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to evaluate it.
  • Integrate exponentials, sine and cosine of linear arguments and reciprocals, including the logarithmic antiderivative of 1/x on an appropriate interval.
  • Recognise a reverse chain rule or choose a one-step substitution. Transform the differential and either change the limits or return to the original variable.
  • Derive integration by parts from the product rule and apply it repeatedly where necessary. Use a partial-fraction decomposition to integrate rational expressions with linear factors.
  • Find intersections and split the interval where necessary to calculate the area enclosed between curves.
