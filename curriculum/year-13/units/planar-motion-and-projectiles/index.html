Year 13 · Spring · Mechanics

Motion in a plane and projectiles

Learning objectives

  • Extend constant-acceleration equations to vector motion in two dimensions. Use components to recover an initial velocity or the position at a common time.
  • Differentiate a vector position to obtain velocity and acceleration, or integrate components with initial conditions. Distinguish speed from a velocity component.
  • Model a projectile’s horizontal and vertical motion under uniform gravity. Find time of flight, range, greatest height or a trajectory equation, including launches from a height.
  • Select physically admissible solutions and test the effects of assumptions about air resistance, point size and constant gravity.

Specification references Q3, Q4, Q5, J5, E9
