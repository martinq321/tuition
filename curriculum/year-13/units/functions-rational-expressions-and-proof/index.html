Year 13 · Autumn · Pure

Functions, rational expressions and proof

Learning objectives

  • Define a function with its domain and range. Find a composite and its domain, and restrict a domain when necessary to construct an inverse. Relate inverse graphs by reflection in y = x.
  • Sketch the modulus of a linear expression and intersections with other graphs. Apply a sequence of graph transformations, keeping track of their order.
  • Simplify and divide rational expressions while retaining excluded values. Decompose proper fractions with constant or linear numerators into at most three partial-fraction terms with linear factors in the denominator, including factors repeated twice.
  • Prove that √2 is irrational and that there are infinitely many primes by contradiction. Adapt the method to an unfamiliar claim and identify the step that creates the contradiction.
  • Compare candidate functions for a context by inspecting domain, range and end behaviour. Explain which observations would justify changing a model.

Specification references A1, B6, B7, B8, B9, B10, B11
