Year 13 · Spring · Statistics

Testing a mean and testing for correlation

Learning objectives

  • Form hypotheses about a population mean under a Normal model with known or assumed variance. Use the sampling distribution of the mean and distinguish its standard error from the spread of individual observations.
  • Choose a one- or two-tailed test, calculate a probability or use a critical region, and express the evidence about the mean in context.
  • Interpret a supplied correlation coefficient as a measure of linear association. Use a supplied p-value or critical value to test for population correlation.
  • Compare a statistical conclusion with plots of the original data. Discuss the effect of an outlier or a mixture of subgroups before interpreting the result.

Specification references L2, N2, O1, O3
