Year 13 · Autumn · Pure

Sequences, series and binomial approximations

Learning objectives

  • Generate a sequence from an explicit term or a recurrence and investigate whether it is increasing, decreasing or periodic. Translate sums into and out of sigma notation.
  • Find an unknown term, common difference or finite sum for an arithmetic sequence. Explain the sum formula by pairing terms.
  • Find terms and finite sums for a geometric sequence. Determine whether an infinite geometric sum exists and use its value only when the common ratio has magnitude below one.
  • Build a sequence model for regular deposits, repayments or a repeated process. Interpret the indexing and test what happens when a parameter changes.
  • Expand a binomial with a rational exponent about zero, stating the interval of validity. Use the first few terms for an approximation and check it numerically.

Specification references D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6
