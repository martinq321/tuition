Year 13 · Autumn · Statistics

Conditional probability and the Normal distribution

Learning objectives

  • Use a two-way table, tree or Venn diagram to change the reference group in a conditional probability. Calculate P(A given B) from the intersection and check the condition for independence.
  • Compare risk before and after new information. Explain how changing independence or an assumed base rate changes a conditional probability.
  • Relate a Normal density curve to a histogram, its centre and spread, and its two inflection points. Interpret areas as probabilities.
  • Use a calculator for Normal probabilities and inverse Normal problems. Standardise a value, or recover an unknown mean or standard deviation from given probabilities.
  • Choose between binomial and Normal models. Check when a Normal approximation to a binomial is reasonable, apply a continuity correction and compare with an exact calculation.
