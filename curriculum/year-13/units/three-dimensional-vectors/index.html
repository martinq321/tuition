Year 13 · Autumn · Pure

Vectors in three dimensions

Learning objectives

  • Write spatial position and displacement vectors in component form. Find 3D distances, magnitudes, directions and unit vectors.
  • Calculate a resultant, a point dividing a segment or an unknown point from vector information, checking the result geometrically.
  • Test whether three points are collinear or two directions are parallel. Interpret a vector result in a spatial or motion context.
