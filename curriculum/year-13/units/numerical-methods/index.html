Year 13 · Spring · Pure

Numerical roots and quadrature

Learning objectives

  • Bracket a root using continuity and a sign change. Show how a discontinuity can mislead and how an even-multiplicity root can be missed.
  • Rearrange an equation as a fixed-point iteration, calculate its successive estimates and draw staircase or cobweb diagrams between y = x and y = g(x). Use the diagrams to explain convergence, oscillation or divergence.
  • Derive a Newton–Raphson step from a tangent, iterate from a starting value and recognise failure near a zero gradient or convergence to a different root.
  • Approximate a definite integral using the composite trapezium rule. Use the curve’s shape, rectangle bounds or a refined partition to assess the estimate.
  • Report a numerical solution to a contextual problem with justified precision. Bracket the answer or check the approximation independently.
