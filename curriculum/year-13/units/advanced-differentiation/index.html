Curriculum / Year 13

Year 13 · Spring · Pure

Differentiation rules and the shape of a curve

Learning objectives

  • Derive the derivatives of sin x and cos x from first principles in radians. Differentiate trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic expressions, including aᵏˣ and tan kx.
  • Choose and combine the chain, product and quotient rules. Apply them to related rates and find the derivative of an inverse function at a suitable point.
  • Find first derivatives of implicit relations and parametric curves. Use them to locate a tangent or normal and consider where a formula for its gradient breaks down.
  • Use first and second derivatives to describe monotonicity, stationary points and curvature. Justify a point of inflection by a change of concavity.
  • Build an objective function from a practical constraint, test boundary cases and interpret the optimum with its units.
