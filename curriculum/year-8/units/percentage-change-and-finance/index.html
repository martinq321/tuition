Year 8 · Spring

Percentage change and finance

Learning objectives

  • Represent an increase or decrease by a single multiplier and calculate percentage change relative to the original quantity.
  • Find an original amount after a stated percentage increase or reduction and check it by working forwards.
  • Compare discounts and price changes using a common baseline. Explain why equal percentage increases and decreases do not cancel.
  • Model simple interest on an unchanged principal, distinguish the interest from the final balance and interpret the time units.
