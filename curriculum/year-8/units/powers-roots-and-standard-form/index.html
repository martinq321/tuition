Year 8 · Autumn

Powers, roots and standard form

Learning objectives

  • Recognise common powers of 2, 3, 4 and 5. Interpret positive, zero and negative integer powers of a non-zero base.
  • Use square, cube and higher real roots as inverses of powers where defined. Distinguish the principal square root from the two solutions of x² = 9.
  • Distinguish an exact root from a rounded decimal. Use a calculator to approximate roots and check their size against nearby powers.
  • Rewrite large and small numbers in standard form a × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ a < 10, including n = 0, and order numbers given in different forms.
