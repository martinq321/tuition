Curriculum / Year 8

Year 8 · Autumn

Signed fractions and mixed calculations

Learning objectives

  • Choose a common denominator or a decimal equivalent to compare signed fractional quantities.
  • Use the four operations with signed proper fractions, improper fractions and mixed numbers, showing written steps when needed.
  • Evaluate mixed calculations involving brackets, powers, roots and reciprocals. Distinguish (−3)² from −3².
  • Choose a fraction, a decimal or a percentage to simplify a calculation and justify the accuracy of the result.
