Year 8 · Summer

Comparing distributions

Learning objectives

  • Construct grouped frequency tables with clear class boundaries for discrete or continuous measurements.
  • Choose appropriate charts for grouped and ungrouped numerical data and use comparable axes when displaying two distributions.
  • Choose measures of centre and spread to compare groups, interpreting a difference in typical value or variability in the context.
  • Distinguish exact conclusions supported by raw data from estimates based on grouped data; explain the effect of an outlier or a misleading display.
