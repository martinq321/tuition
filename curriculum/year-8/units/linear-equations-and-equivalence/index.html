Year 8 · Autumn

Equivalence and linear equations

Learning objectives

  • Use the distributive law to expand a single term over a bracket, and reverse the process by taking out a common factor.
  • Transform linear equations using the same operation on both sides. Solve equations with brackets, fractional coefficients and unknowns on both sides.
  • Represent an unfamiliar problem by choosing an unknown and forming an equation; check the solution in the original context.
  • Distinguish an identity from an equation true for particular values. Use a counterexample to reject an incorrect simplification.
