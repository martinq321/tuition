Curriculum / Year 8

Year 8 · Spring

Constructions and congruence

Learning objectives

  • Construct a perpendicular bisector and an angle bisector with ruler and compass, explaining how equal radii justify the construction.
  • Construct a perpendicular at a point on a line and from a point outside it. Use it to measure the shortest distance from a point to the line.
  • Draw triangles accurately from specified lengths and angles, identify corresponding sides and angles and test whether the data determine a unique triangle.
  • Use SSS, SAS, ASA and RHS to justify congruence. Explain why three equal pairs of angles can still give triangles of different sizes.
