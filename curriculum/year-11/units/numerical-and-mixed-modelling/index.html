Curriculum / Year 11

Year 11 · Summer

Numerical solutions and connected modelling

Foundation and Higher

  • Choose an equation, diagram or graph for an unfamiliar problem. State assumptions, select a method, then check that the scale, units and precision of the answer make sense.
  • Compare approximate and exact answers in a problem linking measurement, finance or geometry. Explain how a changed assumption would affect the answer.

Higher only

  • Apply a supplied iteration formula with suffix notation to approximate a solution. Check whether successive values settle and verify the result in the original equation.
  • Combine an exact model with measurement bounds to determine whether a conclusion is guaranteed or depends on the accuracy of the inputs.

Specification references N14, N15, N16, A17, A20, A21, R16
