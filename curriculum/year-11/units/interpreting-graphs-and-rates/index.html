Curriculum / Year 11

Year 11 · Summer

Graphs, motion and rates of change

Foundation and Higher

  • Plot distance–time, speed–time and unfamiliar financial graphs from tables or descriptions. Read values from piecewise graphs and estimate when two models cross.
  • Use a straight-line gradient to calculate a rate, with units. Explain how a horizontal segment has different meanings on distance–time and speed–time graphs.
  • Compare constant accelerations using slopes on straight sections of speed–time graphs. Plot a reciprocal relationship, such as journey time against speed for a fixed distance, and interpret its shape.

Higher only

  • Estimate an average rate using a chord and an instantaneous rate using a tangent. Interpret gradients in motion and financial models.
  • Estimate area under a non-linear graph using strips and interpret the units, including displacement from a velocity–time graph.
  • Plot an exponential model from a table or equation, such as the spread of a culture growing by a fixed factor, and interpret what the graph predicts. Recognise exponential curves with positive base.
  • Sketch sine, cosine and tangent for angles beyond one turn in degrees, including the asymptotes of the tangent graph.
