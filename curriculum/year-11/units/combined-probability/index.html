Year 11 · Autumn

Combined events and conditional probability

Foundation and Higher

  • Represent two-stage experiments with tree diagrams, Venn diagrams, sample-space grids or two-way tables. Explain why the branch probabilities from a node sum to one.
  • Multiply along a path and add mutually exclusive paths to calculate a combined probability. Check the answer against a complementary event.
  • Distinguish independence from mutual exclusivity. Update probabilities when sampling without replacement and state the assumptions when using the same probabilities again.

Higher only

  • Calculate and interpret the probability of one event given another using tables, trees or Venn diagrams. Use expected frequencies to compare the original and restricted sample spaces.
