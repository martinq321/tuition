Year 11 · Spring

Comparing distributions and judging evidence

Foundation and Higher

  • Compare two samples or time series using appropriate charts, summary statistics, units and the context. Explain the limitations of the sampling method.
  • Assess a claim based on a chart or scatter plot by considering the scale, sample size, outliers and possible extrapolation.

Higher only

  • Construct and read histograms for grouped discrete or continuous data, with equal or unequal class widths. Use area and frequency density to recover missing frequencies.
  • Plot cumulative frequency against class boundaries and estimate medians, quartiles, proportions and interquartile ranges.
  • Construct and compare box plots, explaining what differences in the median and interquartile range suggest about the groups.
