Curriculum / Year 11

Year 11 · Spring

Geometric proof, circle theorems and coordinates

Foundation and Higher

  • Write a chain of reasons establishing an angle or side relationship using congruence, similarity, parallel lines or special quadrilaterals.
  • Use coordinates, gradients and Pythagoras to test geometric claims and check whether a drawing satisfies its stated conditions.

Higher only

  • Prove and apply circle results for central and inscribed angles standing on the same arc, angles in a semicircle or the same segment, and opposite angles of cyclic quadrilaterals.
  • Prove and use radius–tangent perpendicularity, equal tangents from an external point, the perpendicular from a centre to a chord, and the alternate segment theorem.
  • Use x² + y² = r² for a circle centred at the origin. Find the tangent at a point using its radius, including horizontal and vertical special cases.

Specification references G5, G6, G9, G10, G11, A9, A16
