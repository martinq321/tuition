Year 11 · Spring

Triangle calculations and three-dimensional geometry

Foundation and Higher

  • Select Pythagoras or a trigonometric ratio in a two-dimensional multi-step problem. Mark an auxiliary line and keep exact intermediate lengths where appropriate.
  • Combine a plan or elevation with lengths, surface areas or volumes to recover a missing dimension and give the result in sensible units.

Higher only

  • Identify right triangles in three-dimensional figures to find a space diagonal or angle between a line and a plane.
  • Select the sine or cosine rule for a non-right triangle. Check whether the information permits two triangles when using the sine rule.
  • Use half the product of two sides and the sine of their included angle to find an area or to recover a missing length or angle.
