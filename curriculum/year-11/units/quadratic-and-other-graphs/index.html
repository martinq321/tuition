Curriculum / Year 11

Year 11 · Autumn

Quadratic equations, curves and inequalities

Foundation and Higher

  • Relate the factors of a monic quadratic to its roots, locate intercepts and identify the turning point on its graph. Use a graph for an approximate root when factorising is unsuitable.
  • Sketch or recognise straight, quadratic, simple cubic and reciprocal graphs. State why the graph of y = 1/x has no point with x = 0.

Higher only

  • Expand products of three or more binomials and factorise non-monic quadratic expressions. Rearrange a quadratic equation into a useful form.
  • Complete the square to locate a turning point. Select factorisation, completing the square or the quadratic formula to find exact or approximate roots.
  • Solve a quadratic inequality using critical values and a sign diagram or graph. Give the solution in set notation.
  • Plot the feasible region for linear inequalities in two variables and distinguish included from excluded boundaries.
  • Sketch translations and reflections of a given graph, including changes made inside and outside the function.

Specification references A4, A11, A12, A13, A18, A22
