Year 4 · Summer

Money

Learning objectives

  • Represent the same price in pounds and pence and record amounts such as eight pence as £0.08.
  • Estimate a total, work out the exact bill or change and compare two amounts using both pounds and pence.
  • Solve a two-step money problem that includes a fraction or decimal amount.
