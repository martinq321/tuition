Year 4 · Autumn

Area

Learning objectives

  • Describe area as the amount of surface a shape covers. Use equal square units without gaps or overlaps.
  • Find the area of a rectilinear shape by counting squares, including shapes that can be split into rows or rectangles.
  • Draw two different rectilinear shapes with the same area and explain how the grid proves they match.

Try an investigation

  • Fewest squares

    Tile a rectangle with different-sized squares. Count unit squares to explain why the area stays the same.

    Theme: Tiling and packing
  • Packing lab

    Cover a board with rectangles. Compare the area of the board with the sum of the areas of the pieces.

    Theme: Tiling and packing
  • Polyomino studio

    Build two shapes from five unit squares. Explain how you know their areas are equal.

    Theme: Area and dissection
