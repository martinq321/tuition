Year 4 · Spring

Multiplication and division B

Learning objectives

  • Multiply a two- or three-digit number by one digit in a written layout. Explain the value carried from each column.
  • Split a factor into tens and ones, then add the partial products. Show why this gives the same result as a written multiplication.
  • Use known facts and place value to multiply or divide mentally, including cases such as 720 ÷ 9.
  • Choose calculations for integer scaling, multiplication followed by addition, and correspondence problems such as matching any of four tops with any of three skirts.
