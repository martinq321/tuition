Year 4 · Summer

Shape and symmetry

Learning objectives

  • Sort and compare triangles and quadrilaterals by their sides, angles and sizes, including examples turned into unfamiliar positions.
  • Identify acute and obtuse angles. Order angles up to a straight angle, using a right angle as a reference.
  • Find each line of symmetry in a 2-D shape, including diagonal lines when the shape is rotated.
  • Complete a figure on the other side of a given mirror line and check corresponding distances.

Try an investigation

  • Pixel art

    Draw half a pattern beside a mirror line. Predict three reflected pixels before switching on the mirror.
