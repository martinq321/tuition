Year 2 · Autumn

Place value and comparing numbers

Learning objectives

  • Read and write numbers to at least 100 in figures and words. Build two-digit numbers and explain the value of each digit, including a zero in the ones place.
  • Split tens and ones in two ways, such as 42 = 40 + 2 = 30 + 12. Use the second split to solve a practical problem.
  • Represent a number with objects, a diagram and a number line. Estimate where it lies between labelled endpoints.
  • Order numbers from 0 to 100 and record comparisons with <, > or =. Count in 10s forwards and backwards from any number.
