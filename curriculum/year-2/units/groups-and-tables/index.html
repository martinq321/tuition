Curriculum / Year 2

Year 2 · Spring

Equal groups and multiplication tables

Learning objectives

  • Count forwards and backwards along sequences starting at zero in steps of 2, 3 or 5. Use pairs to identify odd and even numbers.
  • Build arrays and connect repeated addition to a multiplication statement with × and =. Turn an array to explain why the factors can change places.
  • Recall products in the 2, 5 and 10 tables and use them to solve an equal-groups story mentally.
