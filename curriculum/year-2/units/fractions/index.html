Curriculum / Year 2

Year 2 · Summer

Fractions of shapes, lengths and sets

Learning objectives

  • Split lengths, shapes and collections into equal parts. Find and name a half, a third or a quarter and record it with a fraction.
  • Find two quarters and three quarters by counting quarter-sized parts. Keep the whole fixed when comparing the fractions.
  • Show with the same strip or set why two quarters equals one half. Write a fraction calculation for a quantity, such as ¼ of 12 = 3.
