Curriculum / Year 2

Year 2 · Spring

Mass, capacity and temperature

Learning objectives

  • Choose grams or kilograms and estimate a mass. Read a balance or scale to the nearest appropriate unit.
  • Choose millilitres or litres, estimate a container’s capacity and read a measuring vessel. Compare masses and capacities in matching units using <, > and =.
  • Estimate which of two temperatures is higher, then read a thermometer and record the temperature to the nearest degree Celsius.
