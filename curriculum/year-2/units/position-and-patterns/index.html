Curriculum / Year 2

Year 2 · Summer

Position, directions and repeating patterns

Learning objectives

  • Describe a straight-line movement from a stated starting point, using left, right, forwards or backwards.
  • Use clockwise and anticlockwise to give quarter-, half- and three-quarter-turn instructions. Link each quarter-turn to one right angle.
  • Arrange shapes or objects in a repeating sequence, including shapes that turn. Explain the rule and predict a missing term.

Try an investigation

  • Robot trails

    Tell a partner a route using forward steps and quarter turns. Try it and repair one instruction if needed.
