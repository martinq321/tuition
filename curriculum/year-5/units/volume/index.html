Year 5 · Summer

Volume and capacity

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish the space occupied by an object from the amount a container can hold.
  • Estimate and compare volumes by building cube arrangements, reasoning about cubes hidden in lower layers.
  • Estimate capacity using familiar containers, then check the estimate against a measured amount.
