Year 5 · Spring

Decimals and percentages

Learning objectives

  • Read, write, compare and order decimals with up to three places. Relate one thousandth to a tenth and a hundredth.
  • Write decimals as fractions with denominators 10, 100 or 1,000, including values with zeros such as 0.407.
  • Round a decimal with two places to the nearest whole or nearest tenth. Solve problems involving numbers with up to three decimal places.
  • Explain a percentage as a count per hundred. Write the same amount as a percentage, a fraction over 100 and a decimal.
  • Use decimal and percentage equivalents of ½, ¼, ⅕, ⅖ and ⅘ in problems. Find equivalents for other fractions whose denominator is a multiple of 10 or 25.

Try an investigation

  • Memory pairs

    Use the fraction–decimal cards. Explain one match using tenths or hundredths and name the equivalent percentage.
