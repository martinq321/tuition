Year 5 · Spring

Perimeter and area

Learning objectives

  • Measure or calculate the perimeter of a composite rectilinear shape in centimetres or metres. Reconstruct missing side lengths.
  • Choose square centimetres or square metres and calculate the area of rectangles, including squares.
  • Compare rectangular areas and estimate an irregular area using a grid. Explain the treatment of partial squares.

Try an investigation

  • 36 fences

    Hold the perimeter fixed and record the area of each possible whole-number rectangle. Which encloses the greatest area?

    Theme: Area and dissection
  • Cut & rearrange

    Cut and rearrange a shape. Compare the area and the perimeter before and after.

    Theme: Area and dissection
