Investigation pathways

Open a theme for its suggested activity order. You can start at any step. Open a status to see what was checked.

Deduction and comparison 3 activities

Use a match, a weighing or a clue to narrow down possible answers.

  1. Memory pairs

    Match visible representations in a small four-pair deck and describe the relationship.

    Source reviewed

    choose checks paired identities and preserves matched positions.

  2. Mystery weights

    Compare two unknown weights, record which is lighter and arrange four objects.

    Source reviewed

    Session.sortTrace generates selection/insertion comparisons.

  3. Mastermind

    Match colours and use a single clue to rule out a guess; begin with fewer holes and colours.

    Source reviewed

    score/filter/partitions implement clue-based elimination.

Sets, combinations and chance 3 activities

Classify cards by their attributes, then reason about combinations.

  1. Find a SET

    Choose three cards whose features are all the same or all different, one feature at a time.

    Source reviewed

    Session.setDeck builds 3^d distinct vectors.

  2. Dobble

    Make three cards using stickers, then check every pair shares exactly one symbol.

    Logic checked

    Recorded checks cover finite-plane decks and pair matches in a simulated document. Physical-device play has not been verified here.

  3. Which hand wins?

    Compare repeated numbers and ordered ranks using example hands.

    Source reviewed

    Core.rank5 handles straights including low ace and tie breakers.

Winning positions 3 activities

Choose a move, predict a reply and look for a strategy that repeats.

  1. 15 game

    Take numbers 1–9 and find three that total 15 using physical number cards if useful.

    Source reviewed

    Session.winningTriple enumerates triples.

  2. Take-away strategy

    Take one or two counters and predict who takes the last; change one rule.

    Source reviewed

    Session.nimWins computes win/loss positions by dynamic programming.

  3. Which card stays?

    Lay out a few cards in a circle and alternate keeping/removing; predict the survivor.

    Source reviewed

    Circle player/card state is separate from survivor display.

Sequences of moves 3 activities

Plan a route through constrained states and compare the moves needed.

  1. Goat & boat

    Carry one item, predict what can safely stay together, and explain why an empty return might help.

    Source reviewed

    crossingMoves and unsafe generate legal states.

  2. Tower of Hanoi

    Move two or three discs legally and notice which smaller discs must move first.

    Browser checked

    2026-11-18 · Select peg A then C, undo, 3 and 8 discs; golden discs/pegs; 390 px iframe control wrap and peg selection.

  3. Twisty cube

    Turn a face, reverse it and compare the coloured net with a physical cube.

    Source reviewed

    Core.turn rotates face-layer positions and normals.

Routes and networks 2 activities

Compare routes through a village, then draw paths subject to network rules.

  1. Magic village

    Choose a project, move cargo or plan a short route and earn in-world gold.

    Source reviewed

    Route permutations and cargo shortest-path search are implemented.

  2. Sprouts

    Join two dots without crossing and add a new dot; begin with one or two dots.

    Prototype

    The game checks drawn routes; players agree when no legal move remains. No complete legal-move solver is implemented.

Tiling and packing 3 activities

Cover rectangles with squares, compare packings and investigate repeated square removal.

  1. Fewest squares

    Cover a small rectangle with whole squares, count them and try to improve.

    Browser checked

    19 November 2026 · In desktop Chromium, Enter placed a 2 × 2 square on a 3 × 5 board, Undo reversed it and the next activity link opened Packing Lab. A separate controller test completes and saves a tiling. Physical-tablet play has not been checked.

  2. Packing lab

    Place rectangles or whole squares in a box and compare arrangements.

    Browser checked

    19 November 2026 · In desktop Chromium, Rotate and Enter placed one of four pieces. See a packing showed a four-piece result with the first diagram and step controls. A separate controller test completes the board and replays the solution. Physical-tablet play has not been checked.

  3. Square it up

    Remove the largest square that fits a rectangle and keep going.

    Source reviewed

    Largest-square subtraction updates remainder and records divisions.

Area and dissection 4 activities

Fix a boundary, join squares and rearrange shapes to compare their areas.

  1. 36 fences

    Use a fixed number of fence pieces to enclose a garden and count its squares.

    Source reviewed

    Boundary checks reject branches/open paths.

  2. Polyomino studio

    Join squares edge to edge, rotate a piece and decide whether it is new.

    Source reviewed

    Canonical key removes rotational/reflected duplicates.

  3. Cut & rearrange

    Cut a shape into pieces, slide/turn them and cover a target.

    Source reviewed

    Cuts split polygons.

  4. Paper surprises

    Predict what a paper loop will do, cut a real strip and compare the result.

    Source reviewed

    Prediction notebook and comparison explanations exist.

Curves and geometric models 5 activities

Construct circles, trace moving points and investigate curved paths and surfaces.

  1. Circle lab

    Drag a point on a semicircle and measure the angle; compare many examples.

    Source reviewed

    Drag models and optional measurement/guides update together.

  2. Chase & escape

    Make points chase one another, change a speed and describe the traces.

    Source reviewed

    visual.js towards/tick advance positions.

  3. Curve lab

    Build a ramp from points, race a ball against a straight slope and predict which wins.

    Source reviewed

    race and numerical path-time functions drive comparisons.

  4. String lab

    Join straight strings, twist a rod tower and notice the curved surface.

    Source reviewed

    lab.js has separate nail/hyperbola/saddle/tower models.

  5. Inside the gallery

    Rotate and shrink repeated picture frames and notice similar copies.

    Logic checked

    Recorded checks cover the complex identity, the excluded origin and gallery modes in a simulated document. The checks do not cover rendering on a physical device.

Spatial structures 3 activities

Inspect cube layers, construct frameworks and investigate a passage through a solid.

  1. Painted cubes

    Paint a large cube, rotate it and sort small cubes by how many painted faces they have.

    Source reviewed

    Small-cube face flags determine classification.

  2. Bridge lab

    Build triangles between supports and observe what changes when a load moves.

    Source reviewed

    The audited editor starts from truss templates. Test the design and simulation together on the intended device.

  3. Cube through a cube

    Slide a cut-out from a yellow cube, then pass a blue cube through and inspect the hole.

    Browser checked

    2026-11-18 · Cube renderer visible, cut-out slider to End, blue cube Halfway, keyboard rotation; blue/yellow and light scene. WebGL versus CPU fallback not separately forced.

Products and factors 6 activities

Start with multiplication facts, build arrays and cuboids, then reason about factors and divisors.

  1. Times tables

    Choose a small group of facts, use an array when stuck and explain a division fact.

    Browser checked

    19 November 2026 · Desktop Chromium confirmed correct keypad feedback and score for 5 × 3. After Start at 820 × 1180, 1180 × 820 and 390 × 844 CSS pixels, the question and full keypad stayed visible, the answer field stayed read-only and the page fitted the width. Physical-tablet input remains untested.

  2. Factor shapes

    Adjust two or three whole-number sides until the area/volume reaches a chosen target.

    Source reviewed

    Save requires exact product.

  3. Explore 30 cubes

    Use 30 small cubes to make boxes and record their side lengths.

    Source reviewed

    Only exact volume 30 saves.

  4. Factor trees

    Split a composite number into two factors and continue until every leaf is prime.

    Source reviewed

    Split requires both factors greater than one and correct product.

  5. Multiply to the target

    Multiply a shared running number by a choice from 2–9 and try to reach a target.

    Source reviewed

    Core.winningMultiplier recursively finds winning moves with memoisation.

  6. Divisor game

    Choose a number and identify which remaining proper divisors go to the other player.

    Source reviewed

    Core.divisors excludes the chosen number itself.

Patterns and change 4 activities

Look for patterns in a table, combine values and predict the next result.

  1. Number patterns

    Click squares to mark a pattern, then compare multiples, squares or primes.

    Browser checked

    2026-11-18 · Switch to remainder colours and numbers + remainders; purple/yellow visible; 390 px iframe responsive controls.

  2. Different totals

    Fill cells with −1, 0 or 1 and compare row and column sums.

    Source reviewed

    strategy.js recomputes row/column totals and checks uniqueness after cell edits.

  3. Add to your neighbours

    Remove a middle number and add it to both neighbours; compare final totals.

    Source reviewed

    Core.remove disallows endpoints.

  4. Money plants

    Grow a seed, harvest coins and keep a day-by-day table with small starting numbers.

    Source reviewed

    advance separates daily phases.

Pictures and rules 3 activities

Use cells, parts and repeated rules to build a picture or character.

  1. Pixel art

    Paint squares and make a mirror image or repeating pattern.

    Source reviewed

    visual.js paint modifies cells and mirror counterparts.

  2. Build a robot

    Choose body parts, vary sizes and describe what changed.

    Source reviewed

    Body/limb proportions and palette controls update drawing.

  3. Creature studio

    Add body segments and pairs of legs, then draw a route for the creature.

    Source reviewed

    Route sampling drives body orientation.

Story worlds 3 activities

Arrange characters and objects, compare sizes and build a scene to describe.

  1. Shark family

    Build a family, compare sizes and add underwater objects.

    Source reviewed

    Character and scenery controls alter scene objects.

  2. Dinosaur world

    Arrange dinosaurs from small to large or create a repeating landscape.

    Source reviewed

    Character drawing options and scene-object controls update saved scene state.

  3. Dragon world

    Create a dragon scene, compare wings and bodies and describe positions.

    Source reviewed

    Object editing, scene creation and save/export controls are wired.

Programs and instructions 3 activities

Sequence commands for a route, debug a grid program and follow a tape machine.

  1. Robot trails

    Put forward and turn pieces together to collect stars, then wrap a repeated section in a loop.

    Source reviewed

    Program interpreter handles repeat blocks and height changes.

  2. Code grid

    Tap commands to move an emoji to a goal; use a short visible loop.

    Source reviewed

    Command blocks form nested instruction structures.

  3. Turing tape

    Act out a simple tape rule, step once and say what changed.

    Source reviewed

    TapeMachine.step applies write/move/next transitions.

Codes and ciphers 3 activities

Rearrange a message, explore substitution and compare a changing cipher.

  1. Scytale

    Wrap a message strip around a cylinder, then unwrap and compare the letter order.

    Source reviewed

    Wrap parameter maps positions to cylinder/flat strip.

  2. Secret messages

    Shift letters around a wheel and decode a short message.

    Source reviewed

    words.js shift normalises letter offsets modulo 26.

  3. Enigma explorer

    Type one letter, trace its path and compare what happens after a rotor steps.

    Source reviewed

    Update reprocesses from starting settings.

Word patterns 2 activities

Build words from a letter set and use positional clues to rule out guesses.

  1. Spelling Bee

    Build words using a fixed set of letters including a required centre letter.

    Published word game

    Published as a word game; curriculum alignment has not been reviewed.

  2. Five-letter words

    Use a letter clue to rule out a proposed five-letter word.

    Published word game

    Published as a word game; curriculum alignment has not been reviewed.
