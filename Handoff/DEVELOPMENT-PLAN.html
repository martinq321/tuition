Maths Wednesdays: from working activities to a commercial service

Use the task IDs below to assign bounded jobs to another instance. “Partially complete” means code exists but its full acceptance criteria have not been met. This package is a static offline snapshot; the platform pilot is a separate runnable project.

Recommended hierarchy

  1. Know what exists: T01 inventory and T02 real-device smoke tests.
  2. Make the teaching activities dependable: T03–T10, starting with those used next in tutoring.
  3. Make the experience coherent: T11–T12 design/accessibility. Start with representative pages before a whole-site overhaul.
  4. Make learning pathways explicit: T13–T17 curriculum, practice, goals, assessment and reviewed reporting.
  5. Make access genuinely controlled: T18–T22 hosting, authentication, admin permissions, migration and tuition rooms.
  6. Build evidence and a viable service: T23–T28 payments, professional review, pilots, school training and launch.

Design, curriculum and hosting decisions can progress alongside activity fixes. Do not migrate every page before the shared design and permission model are settled.

Timescale and outsourcing

Estimates below are rough active-work planning ranges for an experienced implementer, not promises or quotes. A narrow tutoring pilot may take 4–8 focused weeks after triage. A credible small commercial service is more plausibly a 6–12 month staged project, with school evidence extending beyond that. Solo part-time work can take substantially longer.

Prioritise specialist help for a small visual/interaction design system, authentication/payment review, child-data/safeguarding review, and a device/accessibility audit. Commission sample pages with acceptance criteria before a full aesthetic overhaul. Request quotations for defined deliverables; prices have not been researched for this handoff. AI can help apply an approved design, but needs regression checks.

Educational model

Interactivity plus hands-on investigation, followed by suitable consolidation; learner choice, manageable challenge and explanation rather than speed alone. Tutors may assign consolidation as parent-supported homework. Avoid turning the product into endless compulsory screen practice. Build constantly, select and reject, then develop the activities that prove worthwhile.

Use educational support evidence responsibly. Neither attachment labels nor ACE scores should be inferred from gameplay. A creative activity may support engagement or expression without being represented as art psychotherapy. If educational psychology becomes part of the professional route, keep commercial evaluation and professional assessment roles clear.
